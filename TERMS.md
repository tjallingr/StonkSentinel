## 2. Permitted use

Use is restricted to the operator, on the operator's own financial accounts. The
application is not licensed or offered for commercial use, resale, or use on behalf
of third parties.

## 3. What the application does

It reads balances, positions and foreign exchange rates from the operator's own
accounts via authorised read-only interfaces, stores them locally, and presents
summaries, historical charts, performance figures and projections.

## 4. What the application does not do

- It does not initiate payments or transfers
- It does not place, modify or cancel trades
- It does not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice
- It does not share data with any third party

## 5. No advice, no reliance

All figures, returns and projections shown are informational and may be
incomplete, delayed or wrong. Projections are model outputs based on assumptions
chosen by the operator; they are not forecasts and carry no predictive weight.
Nothing produced by this application should be relied upon for any financial
decision. Authoritative balances and positions are those reported by the relevant
bank or broker.

## 6. Accuracy and availability

The application is provided as-is, with no guarantee of accuracy, completeness,
timeliness or availability. Data sources may fail, rate-limit, or return
inconsistent values. Bank access consents expire and require periodic
re-authentication, during which figures will be stale.

## 7. Access authorisation

Access to account information requires the operator's explicit consent, granted
directly to the relevant bank through Strong Customer Authentication. Consent is
time-limited, read-only in scope, and may be withdrawn at any time through the
bank's own channels or by deleting the stored session.
